How it works

A simple way to keep track of your food.

Add your food, see what needs using, and keep your fridge and freezer up to date.

01

Add an item

Add the name and category, then choose whether the item belongs in your fridge or freezer. For fridge items, add an expiration date.

02

See what needs using

Each fridge item changes color as its expiration date gets closer.

A quick guide to the colors:

Expires within 3 days Expired

03

Remember what's frozen

Keep a simple list of what is in your freezer, so food does not disappear at the back.

04

Update as you go

Tap an item to edit its details or move it between the fridge and freezer. Swipe it for a quick delete once you've used it.
